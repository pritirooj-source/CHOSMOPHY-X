## Mission Executive Summary
**CosmoPhy-X** is an operational computational medicine system designed to predict, monitor, and mitigate deep-space pathophysiological hazards in real-time. By bridging NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR) spaceflight multi-omics profiles with extreme extremophile biophysical pathways, CosmoPhy-X autonomously synthesizes clinical countermeasure formulations and health monitoring protocols for long-duration interplanetary missions.

## Mathematical Formulations

### 1. Baseline Risk Index ($BRI$)
$$\text{BRI} = \text{clamp}\left( R_{\text{base}} + (\Phi_{\text{rad}} \cdot t \cdot \alpha_{\text{rad}}) + (\mu_g \cdot 18.0 \cdot \beta_{\text{grav}}), \, 0.1, \, 99.0 \right)$$

Where:
- $R_{\text{base}}$: Intrinsic pathology vulnerability baseline[cite: 1].
- $\Phi_{\text{rad}}$: Solar particle and Galactic Cosmic Radiation (GCR) daily flux ($\text{mSv/day}$)[cite: 1].
- $t$: Mission exposure duration in Sol days[cite: 1].
- $\mu_g$: Microgravity differential factor ($\max(0.0, 1.0g_{\text{dest}})$)[cite: 1].
- $\alpha_{\text{rad}}, \beta_{\text{grav}}$: Multi-omics pathway sensitivity coefficients[cite: 1].

### 2. Mitigated Risk Index ($MRI$)
$$\text{MRI} = \text{clamp}\left( \text{BRI} \cdot (1.0 - \eta_{\text{eff}}), \, 0.1, \, 99.0 \right)$$

Where $\eta_{\text{eff}}$ represents the empirical countermeasure suppression efficacy derived from the CosmoPhy-X extremophile biocatalytic matrix.
